Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 13, 2026

HuePic is a small, friendly game about noticing color in the everyday world around you. These guidelines exist so everyone's daily photo feels safe to open. They apply to every Find you publish, every report you file, and every interaction with friends and Friend Groups.

What you can publish

Photos of the world around you, in today's color — that's it, that's the game. Be thoughtful about photographing other people: get their okay first if they're clearly the subject of your photo, and never photograph someone in a private moment or private space without their knowledge.

What's never okay

  • Sexual or exploitative content, including any content involving minors in any way. This is never tolerated, ever, and will result in immediate account action and be reported to the relevant authorities where required by law.
  • Violent or graphic content — gore, real-world violence, or content that glorifies harm to people or animals.
  • Harassment or bullying of anyone, including other HuePic users.
  • Hateful content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar characteristics.
  • Threats of violence or harm against any person or group.
  • Illegal activity, or content depicting or promoting it.
  • Impersonation of another person, or misrepresenting who you are.
  • Spam — repeated, irrelevant, or promotional content that isn't a genuine attempt at the daily challenge.
  • Violations of someone else's privacy or intellectual property — publishing a photo you don't have the right to share, or that identifies someone without their consent.
  • Attempting to bypass or trick our automated moderation — for example, publishing an image crafted specifically to slip past screening.

How this is enforced

  • Every published Find is automatically screened by an image-moderation service before your friends can see it. If a photo doesn't pass, it's never shown to anyone — it stays private to you in your own history, and you can try again.
  • You can report any Find you see from a friend, right from the app — tell us why, and add a note if it helps. Reports go to us for review; the person you report is never told who reported them.
  • You can block anyone, at any time, from Settings or directly from their profile — a blocked person immediately stops seeing your activity and you stop seeing theirs.
  • We review reported content and accounts and may remove content, and warn, suspend, or terminate an account, for violating these guidelines — proportionate to what happened, with repeated or serious violations (especially anything involving minors, threats, or illegal content) leading to immediate, permanent removal.

We don't currently commit to a fixed review turnaround time (like "within 24 hours") — reports are reviewed by our team, not an automated queue, so response time can vary. If something feels urgent or unsafe, use the in-app report and also reach out through Support directly.

The spirit of it

HuePic works because it's a low-stakes, genuinely fun way to notice the world with friends. If you're ever unsure whether something's okay to publish, a good rule of thumb: would you be comfortable if the person in the photo, or the friend seeing it, knew exactly how and why you took it? If not, don't publish it.

Questions

If you're unsure whether something's allowed, or want to appeal an account action, contact us — see Support.
