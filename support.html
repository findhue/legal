What is FindHue?

FindHue is a daily color-matching photo game: every day, everyone in the world gets the same color to find. Take a photo of something in that color, see how close you got, and share your best find ("your Find") with friends.

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us: support-findhue@edumon.studio.

We read every message, including bug reports, account issues, content reports you'd rather not file in-app, and appeals of an account action.

Camera issues

"Can't share this photo" / the camera won't open. FindHue only asks for camera access the first time you tap Take Photo. If you said no, or turned it off later:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Privacy & Security → Camera.
  3. Find FindHue and turn it on.
  4. Come back to FindHue and try again.

Publishing and network issues

Every photo you take — good, bad, or in between — is saved privately on your device the instant you confirm it, whether or not you ever publish it. So if publishing fails (no connection, or the day's challenge has already ended at 10 PM Eastern), nothing is lost — your attempt stays in that day's history and you can try publishing again, or just try again on the next day's challenge.

"This photo can't be shared" (moderation)

Every photo you publish is automatically checked before your friends can see it. If a photo doesn't pass that check, it's held back — your friends never see it, and it's not lost either: it stays in your own private history like any other attempt, and you can try again with a different photo. If you think a photo was blocked in error, contact us above.

If instead you see "We couldn't check this Find right now," that means our moderation service was temporarily unreachable — your photo is still saved privately, and you can try publishing again in a bit.

Friends and invite codes

  • Adding a friend: open Friends → the "+" icon, then either enter their 6-character invite code or scan their QR code.
  • Groups: Friend Group invite codes are 8 characters, and work the same way (enter or scan).
  • Code not working? Codes are case-insensitive but must be entered exactly — double check for a mixed-up 0/O or 1/I. Regenerating your own invite code invalidates the old one.
  • Blocked someone by accident? Settings → Blocked people → Unblock. Unblocking doesn't restore the friendship automatically — you'll need to add each other again.

Rewarded ads ("Watch an ad" for extra attempts)

  • Everyone gets 3 free Find submissions per day; watching a short ad unlocks another one.
  • "No ad available right now" just means Google didn't have an ad to show at that moment — this is normal and not specific to your device; try again in a bit.
  • Watched the ad but didn't get credit? Verification can take up to about a minute after the ad finishes — if it's been longer than that and you're connected to the internet, contact us.
  • Something in an ad seemed inappropriate or age-inappropriate? Let us know — see Contact us above — including roughly when you saw it, so we can follow up with our ad provider.

Notifications

FindHue can send you one local reminder a day (Settings → Reminders) — it's generated entirely on your device, so it works even offline, and always shows that day's real color. If it's not arriving:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Go to Notifications → FindHue.
  3. Make sure notifications are turned on.
  4. In FindHue, confirm Settings → Reminders is switched on with a time set.

Deleting your account

  1. Open FindHue → tap the gear icon (Settings).
  2. Scroll to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

This permanently removes your account, friend connections, groups, reports, blocks, and published Finds — it can't be undone. This only appears once your device has a real backend connection; if you'd like your locally-saved history removed instead (without deleting an account), use Settings → Clear all records, which stays on this device and doesn't affect anything already published to friends. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what each of these does.
