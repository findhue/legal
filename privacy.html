Privacy Policy

Note: this policy was written by inspecting HuePic's actual production code, database schema, and server functions — it's an accurate, evidence-based description of what the app does today, not a substitute for legal advice.

Who we are

HuePic is made by EduMon Studios. If you have questions about this policy or your data, see Contact below.

The short version

HuePic is a daily color-matching photo game you play with friends. Most of what HuePic knows about you lives only on your own device. The only things that ever leave your device are: an anonymous account identifier, the display name and Find photos you actively choose to publish to friends, your friend/group connections, reports you file, and (if you allow it) analytics/crash data and ad requests. We don't sell your data. The sections below explain exactly what's collected, why, and who else can see it.

Information you provide directly

  • Display name and avatar. You can set a display name (shown to friends) and a profile picture. Your avatar photo stays on your device only — it is never uploaded anywhere. Your display name is stored on our server (Supabase) so friends can see who's who.
  • Sign in with Apple. HuePic works from the moment you open it, with no account or login screen — an anonymous session is created automatically so your progress can sync. If you choose Settings → Save account with Apple, HuePic uses Sign in with Apple to attach a permanent identity to that same anonymous account (so nothing about your history changes). Depending on what you allow Apple to share, this can include your name and an email address (which may be an Apple-generated private relay address rather than your real one). We store Apple's own stable, per-app identifier for your account for reference; we do not use it for anything beyond making sign-in work.
  • Friend connections. Adding a friend (by invite code or QR scan), accepting or declining a friend request, and removing or blocking someone are all stored against your account so the Friends feature works.
  • Friend Groups. Creating, joining, or leaving a Friend Group, and your membership/role in it, are stored the same way.
  • Reports. If you report another user's Find, we store who filed the report, who/what was reported, the reason you selected, and any note you added. Reports are visible only to HuePic for review — never to other users, including the person you reported.

The daily photo challenge

  • Every attempt is saved to your device, whether or not you ever publish it — including photos you retake or never share. This private history never leaves your device unless you explicitly publish a Find.
  • Publishing a Find is a separate, deliberate action ("Make This My Find"). Only the one photo you choose to publish for a given day — never your other attempts — is uploaded to our storage provider (Cloudflare R2), along with its score, the target and captured colors, and where you tapped on the photo.
  • Photos are re-encoded before upload, which as a side effect strips embedded metadata such as GPS location and device information. HuePic does not request or collect your location.
  • Your device never receives a permanent public link to an uploaded photo — only short-lived, expiring download links generated on demand by our server, and only for people who are actually allowed to see that photo (you, or an accepted friend once it's approved and revealed).
  • Published photos are automatically deleted from our storage provider no later than about 72 hours after they're published, regardless of whether your account still exists at that point. The score, date, and color metadata for that day are kept as part of your history (see Retention).

Automated content moderation

Every photo you publish is screened by an automated moderation service (Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety) before it's shown to any friend. The photo's bytes are sent directly from our server to Azure for analysis and are not stored by Azure beyond what's needed to return a result; Azure never receives your name, friend list, or any other profile information — only the image itself. If the service is unavailable or returns something HuePic doesn't recognize, the photo is held back rather than shown (never auto-approved). We keep a record of the moderation outcome (approved/rejected, which category if rejected) tied to that specific photo, for as long as your account exists, so the same rejected photo can't simply be resubmitted.

Friends, groups, and reactions

Your Finds are visible to your accepted friends (and, if you're in one, your Friend Group) only after they're approved by moderation, and only for a short window (the current and immediately prior daily challenge) — HuePic doesn't keep a scrollable public history of your friends' old posts. "Hearts" (reactions) are anonymous to other users — the person you hearted never sees who hearted them — but a heart is still recorded against your account internally, the same way a report is, so the system can enforce "one heart per person per day" and prevent hearting your own Find.

Analytics and crash diagnostics

HuePic uses PostHog for basic product analytics and crash/error reporting, active only in production/TestFlight builds (never during local development). What this does and doesn't include:

  • You're identified to PostHog only by an opaque internal account identifier — never your name, email, avatar, or any other personal detail.
  • Every event HuePic sends goes through an allow-listed vocabulary of specific product events (e.g., "a Find was published," "a rewarded ad was watched") — not a general activity log.
  • A defense-in-depth filter strips anything that looks like a token, password, email address, photo/file URI, invite code, or similar sensitive value from every event and crash report before it's sent, even if it were accidentally included.
  • Session replay, precise location/GeoIP enrichment, surveys, and remote feature flags are all turned off — HuePic doesn't use any of them.
  • Crash reports may include technical diagnostic information (device/OS type, app version, stack traces) needed to fix bugs.

Advertising

HuePic shows ads through Google AdMob in exactly one place: an optional "Watch an ad" option that unlocks extra Find submissions on days you've used your free daily allowance. Ads are never shown anywhere else, and watching one is always your choice — never required to play. HuePic requests only non-personalized ads, does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and does not intentionally access your device's advertising identifier. Before requesting any ad, HuePic runs Google's own consent flow, which shows a consent form where one is legally required (for example, in the EEA/UK) and does nothing otherwise. Confirming you watched an ad all the way through is verified by our server using an opaque request identifier — not any personal information.

Note: AdMob and Google's underlying ad-serving infrastructure are third-party services we don't fully control — see Third-party services below.

Retention and deletion

  • Local device data (your private capture history, avatar, app settings, reminder time) stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.
  • Published Find photos are deleted from cloud storage automatically within about 72 hours of publishing.
  • Find metadata (score, date, target/found color, whether it was published) is kept as part of your personal history for as long as your account exists.
  • Reports, blocks, and moderation records are kept for as long as your account exists, to keep the reporting/blocking system working correctly.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently removes your profile, friend connections, group memberships, reports, blocks, reactions, Find history, and your underlying account itself — not just a deactivation. See Account deletion below for exactly what this does.

Account deletion

Settings → Delete account is available to anyone using the app — you don't need to have saved your account with Sign in with Apple first. It:

  1. Deletes your profile and, through it, every friendship, friend request, group membership, report, block, reaction, and Find record tied to your account.
  2. Deletes the underlying account identity itself (not just the data associated with it), so nothing about you remains sign-in-able afterward.

This can't be undone, and there's no "grace period" — deletion happens immediately when you confirm it. Deleting your account is separate from Settings → Clear all records, which only erases your private photo history stored on this device and does not touch your account or anything already published to friends — use Delete account for that.

Local device data

Your private capture history (every Find attempt, published or not), your avatar photo, your app preferences, and your reminder settings are stored only on your device using standard iOS local storage. HuePic never uploads this local history — only the one photo per day you explicitly choose to publish ever reaches our servers.

Security

Data in transit to and from Supabase, Cloudflare R2, and Azure is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data on our backend is enforced by database-level row-level-security rules scoped to your own account. Photo storage uses short-lived, single-purpose links rather than permanent public URLs. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of any information you transmit to us.

International data processing

HuePic's backend and analytics infrastructure run on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located in the United States or other countries. By using HuePic, you understand your information may be processed outside your own country.

Children

HuePic is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under the age required by applicable law in your region. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.
