FindHue is a small, friendly game about noticing color in the everyday world around you. These
guidelines exist so everyone's Daily Find and Party Find feels safe to open. They apply to every
Daily Find and Party Find you publish or submit, every report you file, and every interaction with
friends, Friend Groups, and Parties.

## What you can publish

Publish a genuine photo taken for today's challenge or for the Party you're playing. The whole
image does not need to consist exclusively of the target color. Be thoughtful about photographing
other people: obtain permission before making someone the clear subject, and never photograph
someone where they reasonably expect privacy.

## Real-world safety {#real-world-safety}

Finding a color is never worth risking injury, breaking the law, trespassing, or endangering
yourself or anyone else. When playing or capturing a photo for FindHue:

- Stay aware of your surroundings, and follow traffic laws, posted signs, property rules, closures,
  and instructions from authorities or property owners.
- Never trespass onto private or restricted property.
- Never use the App while driving, cycling, or anywhere else a phone would be a distraction hazard.
- Never climb, enter roadways or construction areas, or put yourself or others at risk for a Find —
  skip it instead.
- Never damage property, disturb wildlife, or harass or interfere with anyone else while playing.

The full version of this commitment is in the Terms of Use's
[Real-world safety and lawful play](/terms#real-world-safety) section, and Support has a short
[Playing safely](/support#playing-safely) summary too.

## What's never okay

- **Sexual or exploitative content**, child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or the sexualization,
  exploitation, or grooming of minors. Normal, non-exploitative family photos are not prohibited
  merely because a child appears.
- **Graphic violence or gore**, cruelty, or glorification of serious harm.
- **Encouragement, instruction, or glorification of self-harm or suicide.**
- **Harassment or bullying** of anyone, including other FindHue users.
- **Hateful content** that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual
  orientation, disability, or similar characteristics.
- **Threats** of violence or harm against any person or group.
- **Illegal activity**, or content depicting or promoting it — including photos taken by trespassing
  or otherwise breaking the real-world safety rules above.
- **Impersonation** of another person, or misrepresenting who you are.
- **Spam** — repeated, irrelevant, or promotional content that isn't a genuine attempt at the
  challenge or Party.
- **Violations of someone else's privacy or intellectual property** — publishing a photo you don't
  have the right to share, or that identifies someone without their consent.
- **Attempting to bypass or trick our automated moderation** — for example, publishing an image
  crafted specifically to slip past screening.
- **Malicious, false, or abusive reports**, or misuse of safety tools.

## How this is enforced

- **Every Daily Find and Party Find is screened** before anyone else can see it. A genuine
  moderation rejection prevents publication and remains in your private local history. A transient
  moderation or network failure is held back rather than approved; FindHue may retry it when the app
  returns to the foreground. Passing automated moderation is not an endorsement, and automated
  systems can make mistakes.
- **You can report any Daily Find or Party Find** you can see, right from the app — tell us why,
  and add a note if it helps. Reports go to us for review; the person you report is never told who
  reported them.
- **You can block another user** through FindHue's social controls. Blocking restricts relevant
  FindHue activity in both directions, across Friends, Friend Groups, and Parties. Blocked users can
  be managed from Settings, but blocking does not necessarily remove either person from a shared
  Friend Group or in-progress Party.
- **We review reported content and accounts** and may refuse publication, remove content, warn,
  restrict, suspend, or terminate an account proportionately. Serious cases, including child sexual
  exploitation or abuse, credible violence threats, severe harassment, serious unlawful activity,
  deliberate moderation bypass, or significant safety-system abuse may receive immediate action.
  Unlawful material or activity may be reported to authorities where required by law.

We aim to review reports promptly, prioritizing urgent safety, exploitation, and threat reports, but
review time may vary. For immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services and then
use in-app reporting or [Support](/support).

## Appeals

Contact Support to request review of a suspension, restriction, or termination. An appeal may
provide context for reconsideration, but not every appeal will result in reversal.

## The spirit of it

FindHue works because it's a low-stakes, genuinely fun way to notice the world with friends. If
you're ever unsure whether something's okay to publish, a good rule of thumb: would you be
comfortable if the person in the photo, or the friend seeing it, knew exactly how and why you took
it? If not, don't publish it.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether something's allowed, or want to appeal an account action, contact us — see
[Support](/support).
