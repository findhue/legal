## 6. Friend Groups and Parties

Friend Groups are private named circles. Membership does not automatically create direct
friendships, and owners and members may have different management permissions. A Party is a timed,
shared color hunt — either tied to one Friend Group (a Group Party) or opened to anyone with its
invite code or QR (a Quick Party) — that ends at its own scheduled time. Technical and anti-abuse
limits may apply to Friend Groups and Parties. Scores are entertainment and social comparisons, not
scientifically exact measurements: camera hardware, white balance, lighting, image processing,
compression, and point selection can affect results. Hearts, Closest Match, Crowd Favorite, Party
results, and similar recognition have no cash value and are not prizes.
