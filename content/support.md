## What is FindHue?

FindHue is a color-matching photo game: every day, everyone in the world gets the same color to
find. Take a photo of something in that color, see how close you got, and share your best find
(your Daily Find) with friends. You can also start or join a **Party** — a timed, shared color hunt
with a Friend Group or anyone with its invite code — and submit a Party Find before time runs out.

## Contact us {#contact-us}

The fastest way to reach us: [support-findhue@edumon.studio](mailto:support-findhue@edumon.studio).

Use this address for bugs, account issues, safety issues, direct content reports, questions, and
appeals.

## Playing safely {#playing-safely}

FindHue sends you out into the real world to notice color — so play safely. Stay aware of your
surroundings, follow traffic laws, posted signs, property rules, closures, and instructions from
authorities or property owners, and never trespass onto private or restricted property. Never use
the app while driving, cycling, or anywhere else it would be a distraction hazard, and never climb,
enter roadways, or put yourself or anyone else at risk for a Find. If completing a Find would mean
doing something unsafe or not allowed, just skip it — no score or result is worth it. See the full
[Real-world safety and lawful play](/terms#real-world-safety) section of our Terms of Use.

## Camera issues

**The camera won't open.** FindHue only asks for camera access the first time you tap **Take
Photo**. If you said no, or turned it off later:

1. Open your device's **Settings** app.
2. Go to **Privacy & Security → Camera**.
3. Find **FindHue** and turn it on. On some iOS versions you can also use
   **Settings → Apps → FindHue**.
4. Close and reopen FindHue, then try again.

## Publishing and network issues

Every confirmed attempt is saved privately on-device. A failed publish does not destroy it;
temporary network failures may retry while that challenge or Party remains open. For the daily
challenge, once **7 PM Pacific Time** has passed, that day's Find can no longer be newly published
or replaced socially — you can still complete a Late Find for your own private History and
Collection, right up until the day rolls over at midnight Pacific. A Party simply closes to new
submissions once its own timer runs out. Either way, the attempt itself remains in your local
history.

## "This photo can't be shared" (moderation)

Every photo you publish or submit is automatically checked before anyone else can see it. If a
photo doesn't pass that check, it's held back — other people never see it, and it's not lost
either: it stays in your own private history like any other attempt, and you can try again with a
different photo. If you think a photo was blocked in error, contact us above.

If instead you see **"We couldn't check this Find right now,"** the moderation check could not
complete. The photo is not shown to anyone else and remains in your private local history; FindHue
may retry automatically when the app returns to the foreground, and you can try again later. A
failure never auto-approves a Find.

## Friends, Friend Groups, and Parties

- **Friends tab:** open the **Friends** tab, then the "+" icon, and enter their 6-character invite
  code or scan their QR code to add a friend. This sends them a friend request; you'll become
  friends once they accept it.
- **Friend Groups:** from the Friends tab, create or join a Friend Group with its own 8-character
  invite code (enter or scan), or with an `https://gofindhue.com/g/...` invite link.
- **Parties:** open the **Party** tab to create or join a Group Party (tied to one of your Friend
  Groups) or a Quick Party (open to anyone with its code). Party invite codes are also 8 characters,
  shareable by code, QR, or an `https://gofindhue.com/p/...` invite link.
- **Code not working?** Codes are case-insensitive. The invite alphabet excludes **0, O, 1, and I**;
  if you see one, it was likely misread or copied incorrectly. Regenerating a code invalidates the
  old one.
- **Blocked someone by accident?** Settings → Blocked people → Unblock. Unblocking doesn't restore
  the old friendship automatically — one person must send a new friend request and the other
  accept it. Shared Friend Group or Party membership is not automatically changed.

## Rewarded ads — Watch an ad for extra Find submissions

- Everyone gets 3 free Find submissions or replacements per challenge day, shared across your Daily
  Find and every Party you play that day; one verified rewarded ad can unlock one additional Find
  submission. Captures, scoring, and private history remain unlimited.
- **"No ad available right now"** usually means Google did not have an eligible rewarded ad
  available for that request. Your saved Find is unaffected; try again later.
- **Watched the ad but didn't get credit?** Server-side verification can take up to roughly one
  minute. Closing early may grant no reward.
- **Something in an ad seemed inappropriate or age-inappropriate?** Let us know — see
  [Contact us](#contact-us) above — including roughly when you saw it, so we can follow up with our
  ad provider.

## Notifications

FindHue has two kinds of notification, each controlled separately in **Settings → Notifications**:

- **Morning and Evening reminders** — a local reminder generated entirely on your device (works
  even offline), showing that day's real color. Morning tells you today's color is ready; Evening
  reminds you if you haven't made a Find yet.
- **Push notifications** — toggle each one individually: **Group Party Created** (a friend started
  a Party in one of your groups), **Party Started** (a Party you joined has started), and **Daily
  Reveal Ready** (your Friends reveal is ready to view).

If notifications aren't arriving:

1. Open your device's **Settings** app.
2. Go to **Apps → FindHue → Notifications → Allow Notifications**. Depending on your iOS version,
   **Settings → Notifications → FindHue** may also be available.
3. Make sure notifications are turned on.
4. In FindHue, confirm **Settings → Notifications** has the reminder or push type you want switched
   on.

## Save account with Apple

FindHue begins anonymously. Settings → Save account with Apple links Apple sign-in to your existing
account; it should not create an unrelated second account or erase current Find or social data.

## Deleting your account

1. Open FindHue → tap the gear icon (Settings).
2. Scroll to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

**Delete account** is permanent and removes the server-side identity and associated social/Party/
account data; cloud-object cleanup may finish asynchronously. If you'd like your locally-saved
history removed instead, use **Settings → Clear all records**, which removes private local history
from that device and is not online account deletion. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for exactly
what each of these does.

## Reporting and appeals

Report a Daily Find or Party Find in the app with a reason and optional note. The reported user is
not told who filed the report. For urgent concerns, contact
[Support](mailto:support-findhue@edumon.studio); for immediate danger, contact local emergency
services. You may also contact Support to appeal a suspension, restriction, or termination; not
every appeal results in reversal.
